- R: nine identities eight times, seven identities seven times; sixteen identities/121 positions.
- U and C each: twenty-five identities four times, seven identities three times; thirty-two identities/121 positions.
- E: one identity 35 times, one 34 times, two 17 times, two 9 times; six identities/121 positions.
- H8 names: Chansey, Magneton, Nidoking, Poliwrath, Zapdos.
- R7 names: Clefairy Doll, Devolution Spray, Impostor Professor Oak, Lass, Pokémon Trader, Scoop Up, Super Energy Removal.

Its 216-pack sequence is a modelling choice, not a discovered factory period. Named source spelling is preserved here, not normalized into a catalog.
