**Energy cards** (Japanese: **エネルギー** _Energy_) are the cards that power a [Pokémon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_(TCG) "Pokémon (TCG)")'s [attacks](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Attack_(TCG) "Attack (TCG)") in the [Pokémon Trading Card Game](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Trading_Card_Game "Pokémon Trading Card Game"). Once per turn, a player can attach one Energy card from their [hand](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Hand "Hand") to one of their Pokémon. Pokémon cannot use an attack without having at least the Energy of the cost of that attack attached to it.

Energy cards provide Energy of a specified [Energy type](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Energy_type_(TCG) "Energy type (TCG)"). In order to meet costs of attacks that include specific types, the type of the Energy attached to the Pokémon must match the type of the Energy in the cost. The exception to this is costs of [Colorless](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Colorless_(TCG) "Colorless (TCG)") Energy, which can be met with Energy of any type.

There are multiple effects that can change these parameters.

- Some cards have effects that allow Pokémon to ignore the Energy in the costs of their attacks, or ignore specific Energy [types](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Type_(TCG) "Type (TCG)") in those costs.
- Cards can allow for attaching Energy from the [Discard Pile](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Appendix:Glossary_(TCG)#Discard_Pile "Appendix:Glossary (TCG)") or [deck](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Deck "Deck") to Pokémon, or even allow for attaching one or more energy from the hand to a Pokémon. (A card's effect attaching Energy from the hand is separate from the once per turn attachment.)

  - Cards can allow for moving Energy already attached to one Pokémon to another Pokémon, or removing that Energy from play fully by sending it to another zone.
- Some effects can change the amount of Energy that a single Energy card provides, or change the type of Energy that an Energy card provides.

There are two different classes of Energy cards: Basic and Special.

## Basic Energy cards

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/a/ad/BasicPsychicEnergyMEEEnergy5.jpg/200px-BasicPsychicEnergyMEEEnergy5.jpg)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:BasicPsychicEnergyMEEEnergy5.jpg) [Basic Psychic Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Psychic_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Psychic Energy (TCG)"), an example of a Basic Energy card.

Basic Energy cards (Japanese: **基本(きほん)エネルギー** _Basic Energy_) are the basic form of Energy cards. They provide one unit of Energy of their specific [type](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Type_(TCG) "Type (TCG)") to the Pokémon they are attached to and grant no other effects. Except for specific [Arceus](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Arceus_(TCG) "Arceus (TCG)") cards with a special ruling released in the [Platinum Series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Trading_Card_Game#Platinum_Series "Pokémon Trading Card Game"), Basic Energy cards are the only cards that players are permitted to have more than four copies of in their decks. These cards do not bear [regulation marks](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Standard_format_(TCG) "Standard format (TCG)") and are always permitted for use in official tournaments unless they have nonstandard card backs.

There are 9 Basic Energy cards: [Basic Grass Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Grass_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Grass Energy (TCG)"), [Basic Fire Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Fire_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Fire Energy (TCG)"), [Basic Water Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Water_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Water Energy (TCG)"), [Basic Lightning Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Lightning_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Lightning Energy (TCG)"), [Basic Psychic Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Psychic_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Psychic Energy (TCG)"), [Basic Fighting Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Fighting_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Fighting Energy (TCG)"), [Basic Darkness Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Darkness_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Darkness Energy (TCG)"), [Basic Metal Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Metal_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Metal Energy (TCG)"), and [Basic Fairy Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Fairy_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Fairy Energy (TCG)"). No Basic Energy card provides only [Colorless](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Colorless_(TCG) "Colorless (TCG)") Energy, because Colorless Energy costs can be paid for by any Energy type. There also are no Basic Energy cards that provide [Dragon](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Dragon_(TCG) "Dragon (TCG)") Energy, because Dragon Energy does not exist within the rules of the game. Instead, the attacks of [Dragon-type](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Dragon_(TCG) "Dragon (TCG)") Pokémon have costs that include a mix of other Energy types.

From the [Base Set](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Base_Set_(TCG) "Base Set (TCG)") through [EX Power Keepers](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EX_Power_Keepers_(TCG) "EX Power Keepers (TCG)") expansions, the TCG featured six Basic Energy cards, one for each of the original types excluding [Colorless](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Colorless_(TCG) "Colorless (TCG)"): [Grass Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Grass_Energy_(TCG) "Grass Energy (TCG)"), [Fire Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fire_Energy_(TCG) "Fire Energy (TCG)"), [Water Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Water_Energy_(TCG) "Water Energy (TCG)"), [Lightning Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Lightning_Energy_(TCG) "Lightning Energy (TCG)"), [Psychic Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Psychic_Energy_(TCG) "Psychic Energy (TCG)"), and [Fighting Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fighting_Energy_(TCG) "Fighting Energy (TCG)"). When the [Darkness](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Darkness_(TCG) "Darkness (TCG)") and [Metal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Metal_(TCG) "Metal (TCG)") types were introduced in [Neo Genesis](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Neo_Genesis_(TCG) "Neo Genesis (TCG)") to tie in with [Generation II](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_II "Generation II") [video games](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_games "Pokémon games"), the Energy of those types could only be provided by Special Energy cards. Six and a half years afterward, the release of [Diamond & Pearl](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Diamond_%26_Pearl_(TCG) "Diamond & Pearl (TCG)") came with new Basic versions of [Darkness Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Darkness_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Darkness Energy (TCG)") and [Metal Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Basic_Metal_Energy_(TCG) "Basic Metal Energy (TCG)"). Basic [Fairy Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fairy_Energy_(TCG) "Fairy Energy (TCG)") was added with the [Fairy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fairy_(TCG) "Fairy (TCG)") type as a whole in the [XY Series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/w/index.php?title=XY_Series_(TCG)&action=edit&redlink=1 "XY Series (TCG) (page does not exist)") following the [Fairy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fairy_(type) "Fairy (type)")-type's debut in the [Generation VI](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Generation_VI "Generation VI") video games.

Basic Fairy Energy is no longer included in expansions starting from [Sword & Shield](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sword_%26_Shield_(TCG) "Sword & Shield (TCG)") following the discontinuation of new Fairy-type Pokémon in the TCG,[\[1\]](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Energy_card_(TCG)#cite_note-swsh-1) but it did receive a [Sword & Shield Series](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/w/index.php?title=Sword_%26_Shield_Series_(TCG)&action=edit&redlink=1 "Sword & Shield Series (TCG) (page does not exist)")-style print in promotional releases such as [Elite Trainer Boxes](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Elite_Trainer_Box_(TCG) "Elite Trainer Box (TCG)"). The card became the first and only Basic Energy card to [rotate](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rotation_(TCG) "Rotation (TCG)") out of the [Standard format](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Standard_format_(TCG) "Standard format (TCG)") when all Fairy types rotated in [2021](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/2021-23_Standard_format_(TCG) "2021-23 Standard format (TCG)").[\[2\]](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Energy_card_(TCG)#cite_note-fairyrotate-2)

The word "Basic" was not capitalized in card effect text until [Scarlet & Violet](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Scarlet_%26_Violet_(TCG) "Scarlet & Violet (TCG)").[\[3\]](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Energy_card_(TCG)#cite_note-sv-3)

## Special Energy cards

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/6/60/DoubleColorlessEnergySunMoon136.jpg/200px-DoubleColorlessEnergySunMoon136.jpg)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/File:DoubleColorlessEnergySunMoon136.jpg) [Double Colorless Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Double_Colorless_Energy_(Base_Set_96) "Double Colorless Energy (Base Set 96)"), an example of a Special Energy card.

Special Energy cards (Japanese: **特殊(とくしゅ)エネルギー**) are cards with additional benefits compared to Basic Energy cards. Unlike Basic Energy cards, Special Energy cards have [regulation marks](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Standard_format_(TCG) "Standard format (TCG)"), and those with the same name are restricted to four per deck.

Special Energy cards only provide Energy of specific types when attached to a Pokémon. Effects referring to Energy types of Energy cards that are not in play therefore cannot apply to Special Energy. For example, [Flaaffy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Flaaffy_(Evolving_Skies_55) "Flaaffy (Evolving Skies 55)") cannot attach [Speed](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Speed_L_Energy_(Rebel_Clash_173) "Speed L Energy (Rebel Clash 173)")[![Lightning](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/0/04/Lightning-attack.png/20px-Lightning-attack.png)](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Lightning_Energy_(TCG) "Lightning") [Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Speed_L_Energy_(Rebel_Clash_173) "Speed L Energy (Rebel Clash 173)") with its _Dynamotor_ Ability. The original Special [Metal](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Metal_Energy_(Neo_Genesis_19) "Metal Energy (Neo Genesis 19)") and [Darkness Energy](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Darkness_Energy_(Neo_Genesis_104) "Darkness Energy (Neo Genesis 104)") cards are an exception to this rule. Starting with [Scarlet & Violet](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Scarlet_%26_Violet_(TCG) "Scarlet & Violet (TCG)"), these effects specifically refer to Basic Energy.[\[3\]](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Energy_card_(TCG)#cite_note-sv-3) This removes potential confusion and disallows further loopholes for Special Metal and Darkness Energy.

Some effects Special Energy cards may provide include:

- Multiple units of Energy on one card
- Multiple types of Energy on one card
