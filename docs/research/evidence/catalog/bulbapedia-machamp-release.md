**Machamp** (Japanese: **カイリキー** _Kairiky_ ) is a [Fighting-type](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Fighting_(TCG) "Fighting (TCG)") Stage 2 Pokémon card. It was first released as part of the [Base Set](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Base_Set_(TCG) "Base Set (TCG)") .

## Trivia
### Origin
Strikes Back's Japanese name is that of **[Counterattack](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Counterattack_(skill) "Counterattack (skill)")** , a [skill](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Skill_(Shuffle) "Skill (Shuffle)") later introduced in [Pokémon Shuffle](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Shuffle "Pokémon Shuffle") .
[Seismic Toss](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Seismic_Toss_(move) "Seismic Toss (move)") is a [move](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Move "Move") in the [Pokémon games](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_games "Pokémon games") that [Machamp](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Machamp_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Machamp (Pokémon)") can learn.
This card's English [Pokédex](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9dex "Pokédex") entry comes from [Pokémon Red and Blue](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Red_and_Blue_Versions "Pokémon Red and Blue Versions") , though it is slightly reworded. The Japanese entry comes from [Pokémon Red and Green](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Red_and_Green_Versions "Pokémon Red and Green Versions") .
