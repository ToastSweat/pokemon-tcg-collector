## Jungle

J1 = Ivy/blank removed; J2 = First Edition error (not Unlimited); J3 = Exeggutor-filled Unlimited. H/R/C exception-name assignments in J2/J3 inherit J1 under the printed “other sheets assumed unchanged” assumption.[1][2][3]
J3 is not confirmed Asia-exclusive; do not mix it with J1 or the uninspected Belgian variant.[1][4]
