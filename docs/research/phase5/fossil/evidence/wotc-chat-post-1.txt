Feb 3, 2000 - Weekly Pokemon TCG Q&A Pokémon Online Help Offered every Thursday 2-5 PST in the TCG Forum of the Wizards.Community chat site! Featuring Master_Trainer from Customer Service Thurs. Feb 3, 2000 Master_Trainer: Are you SEAKING to WEEDLE answers to your ODDISH Pokemon questions no matter how FARFETCHE’D? GEO-DUDE, don’t feel GASTLY, we are here to remove your JINX and lift you out of the MUK with Master_Trainer power! So you know the BEEDRILL, close your MEOWTH, don’t be a SLOWPOKE and type in your questions RAPID(edly). I have a KABUTO with a gold WD on it. What does this mean and ias it worth anything? You have one of the Wizards’s stamped Kabuto’s. It’s a promo card that was given out with an earlier issue of TopDeck. As far as its value, we don’t keep track of those, check out magazines like TopDeck or Scrye. Why are there holo and non-holos in jungle and fossil? After hearing many people’s comments on not wanting to play with their holo cards from base set, we decided to make the rare’s in both forms. So you can keep your holos mint, and play with the non-holo cards. TopDeck reported 65 cards in Team Rocket. Will “Here Come the Rockets!” be in the set? And if so, which rarity? Sorry, we can’t give out a specific card set list yet. To be honest, I don’t even know the full set yet. Clefairy Doll here . . . Situation: Hitmonchan vs. Mime. Hitmo has 2 plus powers attached. He jabs mime for 20. Is this considered a KO, or do plus powers add 20 to the attack itself (for 40) and so fail due to Mime’s wall? Trainer card effects are added before Pokémon powers are figured, so the attack would do 40 total, which Mr. Mime’s Power would negate. I understand that WotC will be switching over to the Japanese Hologram format soon. Will Base Set 2 be the first expansion with the new format, and will there be any other format changes in store? Well, we will be switching foil format soon (running out of the old foil). No official announcements as to what format or exactly when, sorry. Do you know if there are any plans to have Pro Tour type events with large prizes? Or is this the wrong person to ask? Also, will Team Rocket rares be printed in both holo/non holo versions? There are no plans for a Pro Tour yet. We are working on tournament formats for the future though. As for Team Rocket, we don’t have any specific information on that card set yet, sorry. What is the card going to be in the Pokémon TCG for Gameboy? Good question. Wish I knew the answer. Sorry gang, we don’t announce cards or specifics before they are available. Hello, when does the Pokémon mall tour come to northern New Jersey I need to know now? Well, this year’s Mall Tour is still being worked out. When we have specific dates and locations, it will all be listed on our web site. Can the switch card be used to retreat a paralyzed, confused, or sleeping Pokémon? Yes, you can use a switch card to retreat a confused or asleep or paralyzed Pokémon. Does Magmar’s smokescreen affect when the Defending Pokémon evolves? Since Magmar’s Smoke screen attack references the Defending Pokémon, by it evolving, it removes that effect. Is the Birmingham AL tournament DCI sanctioned? Will actual DCI people be there? Just checked my list, only see sanctioned tournaments in Montgomery and Huntsville in Alabama. I would check with the people running it to be sure. Will there be better odds of getting foils in Base Set 2 and Rocket? No, the same odds. Basically a 1 in 3 chance of a foil card in a booster pack. Does Fearow’s Agility attack block Kangaskahn’s fetch? Actually it does not, Fearow’s agility says stops damage done to Fearow, and the Fetch just draws a card. This is the new ruling from R&D. Any idea of plans for DCI accrediting of Pokémon Judges any time soon? I would assume that would go hand-in-hand with the tournament formats in the works? A customer service rep once told me that WotC considers Pokémon primarily oriented towards ‘casual’ play, but how can there be ‘serious’ play without accrediting and professional formats? Good question. There are no plans for official accrediting of Pokémon Judges at this time. Hi. Do you know what the gym card with a Mr.Mime running from a wheel with arms is called; it’s in the gym sets? Sorry, but as earlier stated, we don’t announce cards until they have been released. A lot of buzz about some holo promo Meowth. True. I’ve seen them. Since we have not released those cards yet, I can’t comment on them, sorry. In the upcoming TR series, do evil Pokémon count as separate cards from regulars? Example: I can have four evil Jolteon and four Jolteon in a deck? No, you can only have 4 of a particular named Pokémon max in your deck. There are already several different Pikachu’s out but you can still only have 4 total. Can the switch card be used to retreat a paralyzed, confused, or sleeping Pokémon? Yes, it can, the Trainer (you) plays NOT the Pokémon, a Trainer Card NOT the Pokémon, so it being Paralyzed, etc. does not stop a Trainer Card’s effects (generally). For the evil Pokémon - they actually have different names and it would make sense that you could have more of them in addition to other Pokémon of that name? Well guess what, I’ve just been corrected. The “evil” Pokémon are considered differently named Pokémon and you can have them in addition to the others, sorry. Can online tournaments be sanctioned if the tourney director is a DCI judge? We don’t have any plans for sanctioned online tournaments at this time. Can you Raindance onto a Shifted Venamoth? Yes you could do that. Why did they take out the real mew from fossil (truth please)? The Japanese felt that it shouldn’t have been included in Fossil to begin with ( #151 and all). We wanted it to be readily available for everyone. FREE! When is your next DCI sanctioned tourney? Ok, as for the DCI question, it differs for each tournament. If I use Charizard’s Energy Burn on some Grass energy could I still use Venusaur’s Energy Trans on them, and if Charizard is confused, paralyzed or asleep and he had some energy burned cards on him would they still be burned or would they change back? Ok, 1st if the energy is changed to Fire energy then Venosaur can no longer Energy Trans them (they are no longer grass). If Charizard is paralyzed, etc. then the energy reverts (it also only changes to fire for that turn only). When will the rocket set and base set two be released, and when will the first edition of these sets be released, and will they sell for the same price? Base Set 2 should be out the end of February, Rocket in Mid April. No 1st edition Base Set 2 cards, but Rocket 1st edition will be out the same time as unlimited. As far as prices, stores set whatever price they want for the cards. I heard that DCI is going to ban the cards Computer Search, SER, and Pro. Oak, is that true? There are no plans to ban any cards at this time. Is there a listing anywhere as to the odds of receiving each card when purchasing a booster pack? It’s based on the size of the set and the cards rarity. One rare card in each booster. I mean out of a thousand cards printed, Charizards are printed? Charizard is no more rare than any other rare in the base set. What will the prefixes of rocket cards (example: ‘Evil Jolteon’) be? I’ve heard ‘bad’, evil’ even ‘devil’ in different magazines and such, and how rare is the ‘here comes team rocket’ card going to be? Sorry but that information hasn’t been released yet either:) Again, until a set comes out, we don’t mention specifics, sorry. Does benching a Pokémon end ALL effects, including those of attacks? For example, if I have a Scyther against a Magmar, and Magmar smokes me… I retreat Scyther, and put up another Pokémon, which I then retreats and return Scyther. Does smokescreen no longer apply? I say no, but there have been a lot of arguments about this online… please settle this once and for all. Benching a Pokémon removes the effects of any attacks that reference them. In this case, benching Scyther removed the Smokescreen effect. How many cards are you planning for the Team Rocket expansion? Sorry guys, again that information is not yet available. Wait until the set comes out. The potion card says to remove “2 damage counters”, some people play with 10 and 30 point damage counters. SO: Can a regular potion take off two 30point damage counters or just 20 damage total? And can you place energy on a mysterious fossil? Ok, whenever a card or effect or attack mentions damage counters, it is always assuming a counter to be equal to 10 points of damage. How are you supposed to get surfing Pikachu to play the mini game in yellow? Sorry, we only make the translated Pokémon game cards. Nintendo makes the Game Boy games and could answer that for you. Snorlax’s Pokémon power says that it cant be paralyzed, poisoned, confused, or put to sleep, so why does it say that the power stops working if its paralyzed, confused or put to sleep? Well, say Muk was in play, all Pokémon Powers stop working. Snorlax gets confused, then Muk leaves play, and his power can’t turn back on until he is no longer confused. I have opened booster boxes (tons) and found that the distribution is not random. There is always 1 booster every 4 packs, with 2 breaks in the box. How come WotC tells us the odds are 1:3? When the cards are printed, the number of foils is 1 in 3 packs. Some people get better odds, some get worse. Fri. Feb 4, 2000 - Pokemon Jr. Game Intro and Q&A RPG Hour: Pokémon, Jr. Game Featuring Stan!, game designer TSRO Ginger: Okay, folks, thanks for coming to our Pokémon, Jr. chat, featuring Stan, game designer extraordinaire!! Any words for the people to open with, Stan, or shall we dive right into questions? Stan: Well, I guess I’ll start with a quick overview of what Pokémon, Jr. is… and what it isn’t. Stan: This is a storytelling game for a parent and up to 6 children (ages 6-8). The kids get to play Pokémon Trainers, collecting and battling their Pokémon against one another, trainers in the stories, and the nasty Team Rocket. It’s a very easy game, because it’s supposed to be playable by kids and parents who do not usually play hobby games (like D&D or Magic), there’s definitely roleplaying involved, but this is NOT a typical RPG. Stan: Anyone looking for a “Guide To Pokémon In Your Campaign” book might be disappointed, but those who try it out (those of ALL ages) have found it to be a fast, fun game. TSRO Ginger: Okay, question and answer time. Go ahead, Guest 494. Guest 494: How much will it retail at? Stan: Because it’s a mass market game, we do not have a manufacturer’s suggested retail price on the box, but I’ve seen it in a few major stores (like Toys R Us, and WalMart) selling for between 9 and 12 dollars. TSRO Ginger: MLM? You’re up. MLMartin: Stan!–has Wizards of the Coast considered using games in this format as introductions to any of its other properties and/or licenses, such as Star Wars or even D&D? Stan: Good question! Pokémon, Jr. is the first time we’ve tried such a game concept (easy roleplaying for a LARGE audience). As we worked on it, we realized that it had a much wider application than just Pikachu and his friends, and we’ve talked about how we might use it for other settings. Stan: So far, though, we don’t have any hard and fast plans to use this game for other settings . . . but we ARE talking about it. MLMartin: Stan, sounds good! Stan: I guess we’ll just have to see how successful this game is. 494: Where will it retail? Stan: Well, you can buy it at most national toy stores. I KNOW it’s in most (if not all) Toys R Us, WalMart, and Target stores. And I know you can get it at Wizards of the Coast and Game Keeper retail stores. And many hobby retailers ordered it too, so you should be able to find it any number of places. Stan: If you want help finding a retailer in your area, contact Wizards of the Coast customer service … they’ll be able to give you some clues. 494: Will prices vary store to store? Stan: I think that prices will be the same in all the stores in a single chain, but will vary from chain store to chain store. TSRO Ginger: Does anyone else have a question? TSRO Ginger: Unicorn, go ahead. TSRO Unicorn: Stan, how much prep does a parent need to start a game with the kids? Stan: Another good question! Very little prep time. In fact, we tried to design the game so that you could pick it up and just play right away! In truth, the parent would do well to take 5 or 10 minutes to read the rules, but it’s certainly possible to just play immediately. The BEST thing is that the parent doesn’t need to know ANYTHING about Pokémon in order to play-it’s all there in the book! So they have fun AND look Poke-smart! TSRO Ginger: Arkana, go ahead. TSRO Arkana: My husband and I are both players of D&D. Would this be a good game to try and introduce our kids into RPGs? Stan: Oh yes, certainly. One of our designers came in and told me this story yesterday: It seems his little girl just got the game (she’s too young to play D&D). She was so excited that she made him come in to watch her fight against a tough opponent… then she carried her new Pokémon with her everywhere she went, and she wanted to play again the next morning (at some hideously early hour). That’s pretty much the way I was when I first found D&D. TSRO Arkana: That sounds just like I was when I discovered D&D. Stan: Anyway, she’s now making up stories on her own and pretty much getting ready to be a DM … well, as soon as she can read. TSRO Ginger: (grinning) That’s a cute story, Stan. TSRO Ginger: Do you actually watch the cartoons to get the ideas, or do they have transcripts for you? Stan: I watch them! In fact, after I finish this chat I have to go watch two more tapes for the next project I’ll be working on. TSRO Arkana: Talk about work! Watching cartoons… TSRO Ginger: (solemnly) You have my deepest sympathy, Stan. TSRO Arkana: I’m not much of a Pokémon fan but I may actually look into getting this for my kids. Stan: Arkana, I think you should. It’s a fun game. I’ve wanted to write a game like this for years, and when I finally got the chance I threw myself into it wholeheartedly. And with the help of Bill Slavicsek, we crafted a really focused and fun game (in my humble opinion). TSRO Ginger: Arkana, you really should. It’s a blast. TSRO Ginger: Okay, Stan, any suggestions for me on how to get my kids not to expect the “episodes” to be exactly like the cartoon? Stan: Ginger, hmmmmm . . . tough question. You might try telling them that this happens after the cartoon episode. TSRO Ginger: I shall try that. They are at that perfectionist age, do you know what I mean? Stan: Ginger, I know what you mean. They want to relive the experience of the TV show, but they also want the thrill of not knowing who will win. TSRO Ginger: Welcome to our Pokémon, Jr. chat, Yosuchi. Stan: Hi, Yosuchi! Yosuchi: Hello. So this is a real thing? TSRO Arkana: Yes, it is, Yosuchi. TSRO Ginger: Yosuchi, Pokémon, Jr.? Yes, it sure is. Yosuchi: The Pokémon RPG is based around what? Stan: Yosuchi, it’s a simple storytelling game for parents and kids. A new game system and stories based off the Pokémon TV show Yosuchi: Oh. SO they get to play as Ash, Misty, Brock/Tracy? Stan: Well, Yosuchi, a child gets to pretend that he is a Pokémon Trainer, and he collects Pokémon for himself. Some kids pretend to be Ash or Misty, but most are happier being themselves. Yosuchi: Oh. So how do they play? Stan: When a kid captures a Pokémon, he gets a game card to represent it. He uses that card in combat. It’s a little hard to explain here in a chat room, but it’s very simply at the table. Even non-gamers seem to pick it up very quickly. Yosuchi: Ah. Thanks. I’ve got to go, but I thank you. TSRO Ginger: You should include tips on creating episodes for the non-gaming parents that buy it. Stan: Ginger, I think that TopDeck magazine will be providing new episodes and printing an article about how to write your own. TSRO Ginger: I’ll have to check that out then, thanks. TSRO Arkana: Stan, do you need to collect any cards for this game? Stan: No. All the cards come with the game. It’s an all-in-one package deal. TSRO Arkana: OK. That I can deal with. TSRO Ginger: I really do recommend it, Arkana. It even comes with keen little damage counters. Necro: I hope it’s not gonna cost sky high… I say this because there seems to be some bad price fixing here in Singapore. Stan: No. It usually costs between 9 and 12 dollars. Necro: Pokémon theme decks works out to US$29.40!!! And children are forking out moola to buy them. Yes, this is sort of a complaint that I have always wanted to bring up. Stan: Pokémon, Jr. isn’t collectable. All the cards come in the box! Necro: Great! Gotta check out the local price though. Treiss: Stan, one question from me (not sure if it has been answered): what about those that don’t at all see this cartoon (yes it is true it doesn’t run in Denmark)-how would they learn the game and figure up episodes for Pokémon RPG? Stan: Treiss, the game teaches you what you need to know about Pokémon and the world. After that, making up your own stories is… well… just like making up your own stories! The kids can help you figure out what’s right and what’s wrong. TSRO Arkana: Treiss, I have no knowledge of the Pokémon game. But, if it is anything like the basic sets of D&D, you don’t need any previous knowledge or anything else to play. TSRO Ginger: Arkana is correct. Everything is in the box. It’s cute. But then, I happen to like playing the card game with my kid, so there you go. TSRO Arkana: I’ve heard of many adults getting hooked on Pokémon through their kids. Stan: I work with several people like that TSRO Ginger: I’m not exactly hooked, but I do think the game is fun. And the creatures are darned cute. Necro: I agree… they are C-U-T-E! TSRO Ginger: Adults probably would not want to play the Pokémon, Jr. game by themselves. It’s really basic, but it’s great for kids. Stan: Actually, I would use Pokémon, Jr. to teach roleplaying to people like my parents or cousins. TSRO Ginger: Do we have any more questions, or shall we let Stan go free a bit early? TSRO Ginger: Stan, I had a Pikachu backpack, but my rotten kid stole it from me. TSRO Ginger: Okay, folks, I think we can safely say that there’s not much more to say, except if you have kids, buy the game. I got mine at Toys R Us for ten bucks. And my kid has played it all the way to the end at least three times. He likes getting different Pokémon. So it’s always fun. TSRO Zugran: Ginger, bring it to Gen Con, so we can play it. TSRO Ginger: I think I shall do that, Zugran. TSRO Ginger: We can make Stan be the GM. TSRO Arkana: Is everybody asleep? This is a big difference from last night. Treiss: (chuckles) Nope, Arkana, just waiting for a Pokémon to turn up. TSRO Zugran: No, just listening to another cool game design. Stan: Any questions, RoyalCharizard? RoyalCharizard: No, I’m just watching. Stan: I sure know how to pack ’em in, don’t I? TSRO Ginger: Well, this game is mostly played by non-gamer parents and their little kids. I think most of the customer base is not really conversent with the Ichat site. Stan: Probably not. Some time I’d like to go to one of the Pokémon events (maybe a League site) and see what the kids and parents there think of the game. TSRO Ginger: But we all love Stan, right!! Treiss: Yep, Stan is “da” man. TSRO Zugran: Stan? Oh yes, we love Stan. Stan is the Man. Stan: Awwww … no need to bolster my ego … it’s BIG enough as it is … but thanks for the thought! Treiss: (chuckles) I know we can’t leave because of it, Stan. You know it blocks the exit! TSRO Ginger: Well, is there anything else, or shall we let Stan go get dinner? TSRO Arkana: He’s buying??? *eager look* Stan: Well, if this is what we’re reduced to chatting about, then we ought to call this thing over … race ya back to RPG forum! TSRO Ginger: Works for me. Stan: Stan is not buying dinner. Treiss: Good call, Stan. TSRO Ginger: I now pronounce this chat good and done. Thurs. GEO-DUDE, don’t feel GASTLY, we are here to remove your JINX and lift you out of the MUK with Master_Trainer power! Do you know when the other expansions will come out? Base Set 2 will be coming out at the end of this month, Team Rocket will be released in April; the Gym expansions will be out later this year. I missed the Pokémon Jr. Adv. chat. When will Pokémon Jr. 2 be released? What licensing problems are you having? When will the more advanced version of Pokémon Adventure (RPG) be released? We are still working out schedules and negotiations with Nintendo for the expansions for the Pokémon Jr. Adventure game, so we don’t have specific dates yet, sorry. Is it true that the Base Set 2 expansion might not have 1st edition prints? Since Base Set 2 is a set reprinting cards from the Base and Jungle sets, there will NOT be 1st edition cards for Base Set 2.The next new 1st edition cards will be out for Team Rocket. What happens if the opponent benches the Pokémon after the fossil Magmar uses smokescreen? Since that attack references the opponent’s Pokémon, if he benches it, the effect is removed. Will there be the white star ultra rare card in the team rocket set? Sorry, but we don’t announce what specific cards will be in a set until it is released. Is there going to be any new promo cards? There certainly will be! However, as I just said, we don’t mention specifics until they are released. How come my holo Nidoqueen has no symbol. There are some Jungle Hologram cards that were printed without the jungle symbol. These are real cards but misprints. The error has been corrected on later print runs. Exactly how many first edition base set boosters were released? Sorry, but we do not release print run numbers, sorry. If you have a defender on a Chansey, and you use double edge, is 20 of the damage to Chansey prevented? Yes, it would be. Defender will protect your Pokémon from attacks form your opponent AND from your own attacks while it is in play. Can I lay a double colorless energy and another energy down on the same turn? Are there any plans for banning cards in the game? We have no plans on banning any Pokémon cards. Is the yellow cheek Pikachu (base set) a misprint? Actually, the red cheeked Pikachu is the misprint. How long until you will stop having these Thursday night chats? As long as you want them, we will have them. Will there be new precon decks for base/jungle 2, gym and rocket and what will they be called? Yes, there will be. We can’t be specific about them until the set is out but there will be PCD’s for Base Set 2 and for Rocket. Will there be any new attack side effects (poison, asleep, confuse, paralyze) I thought I had heard something about “scorch”, but I wasn’t sure if it was true There aren’t plans for anything new like this, no. How come you skipped over promo card #6 ? Different Promo cards are released by different companies, it’s hard to keep them in order. Does the cards with darker text mean that they are misprints? Nope, text and coloring changes are normal in different print runs. What plans are there for future Pokémon league giveaways? You join the League to earn your Pokémon badges. Anything else is just extra, it happens when it happens. Does Magmar’s smokescreen still works if I switch Pokémon? Yes, it would. It references the opponent’s active Pokémon. If he benches THAT, the effect ends. Are there any plans for wizards to make their own Pokémon cards not from Japan? There are no plans at this time to do so. Some people are saying, with all the cards and added confusion, Pokémon will be like MTG. Any plans to avoid doing this? Pokémon is much more basic than Magic, think of Pokémon like Checkers and Magic like Chess (but with many more pieces). How come my 1st edition Zapdos has hardly any holo stuff? If you are referring to the 1st edition Fossil Zapdos, it is a misprint. The holo on the top left is missing where there would have been a stage stamp. Will there be a holo us Mew? We cannot comment on unreleased cards, sorry. How much does 1st edition worth right now? Sorry, but we don’t keep track of card prices; a card is worth whatever you can get for it. I have agreed to run our Pokémon League’s first tournament in a month or so.Do you have to be a DCI member to be either the organizer or the head judge? In a related vein, is there a way to become a DCI member online, or only at existing tournaments? If you organize a tournament you automatically get a DCI number. Otherwise, you can get a DCI number through a tournament or through the League. When gold and silver for Gameboy comes out will you make cards for the 152 to 251 Pokémon? We are still involved in the next few sets (Rocket, and Gym sets). That is too far away to comment on at this time. If you have used Scythers swords dance and your opponent uses a gust of wind on your next turn you retreat the active Pokémon and put Scyther back out does it do 30 or 60 If you retreat Scyther, of course, Swords Dance is canceled. The answer is yes but we have no plans on doing so, outside of making it easy to understand. What are the rules on foreign cards in DCI play? You can use cards that are copies of already released English cards only. And you must have at least 1 English copy with you for translation purposes. If one of your Pokémon was poisoned, and a few turns later it is KO’d by the poison, does your opponent take a prize card although the poison KO’d the Pokémon and not your opponents Pokémon? And what if you’re own Pokémon knocks itself out? Yes, he would still draw a prize card. Whenever a Pokémon in knocked out, the other player gets prize cards even if you knock out your own. What’s going to happen to league after all the cities have been traveled to? The League is in over 2700 locations in North America every week. If you mean the Mall Tour it is still being set up for this year. If my opponent has an active Ditto and I switch my Pokémon, is ditto my new Pokémon, or Ditto with 50hp? Ditto always turns into a copy of whatever Pokémon he is facing when he is the active Pokémon (and not paralyzed, confused, or asleep). How long does Porygon’s conversion attack (changing weakness) last? It lasts until the Pokémon it was used on is benched. Is there any plan for a Pokémon Duelist style magazine? TopDeck has articles on Pokémon every month. Why do I have a jungle Electrode with a base set picture? You have a misprint. How many cards will there being the Rocket series and how many will be holos, rares, and etc? Sorry, but we cannot comment on unreleased cards. Will anymore CDs be released with promos like Japan with there 10 promos in one CD? There are no such plans that I know of. Why do you guys make Holo’s and Non-Holos? Why not like the Japanese? In my personal stats center for the DCI website as a legends members, I can review play statistics for Magic the gathering. I cannot appear to also review play history/statistics for Pokémon. Would I get a prize if I knocked out a doll/fossil? No, you wouldn’t. Is there gonna be a Pokémon pro tour type event, ever? We have not announced anything like that yet. Since the tour is so big, locations are based upon a mall’s willingness to host it. If the Pokémon neo cards were released like 2 months after the game gold and silver came out will ours be released after the English gold and silver come out or will we have to wait for rocket gym leader one and 2 before we get the Neo’s? The next sets, this year will be Rocket and the Gym sets. Will there ever be a Pokémon card fan club like in Japan? We have no plans for anything like that at this time.
