Featuring Master_Trainers from Customer Service Thurs. July 6, 2000 Hey gang! Welcome to the new 3-5 PST Pokémon chat! Master Trainer Pat is out so you’ll just have to put up with me alone! First though, the announcements. Fresh from R & D are the announcements: First, How does Agility and Dark Wartortle Mirror Shell interacts? Agility does NOT prevent Mirror Shell from happening, as it does NOT refer to the Defending Pokémon when this attack is done. Secondly, someone asked if there is a shuffling rule for Pokémon. Normally you follow the DCI rules, which allow you to shuffle or cut your opponent’s deck. Thirdly, a definition that may help with future rulings. Any card that provides Energy is considered an Energy card of the type it provides. So if you were to Energy Burn a DCE, it counts as a Fire Energy card that provides 2 fire Energy until the end of the turn. So it could be gathered by Team Rocket Charmander but would only provide 1 coin flip for the Continuous Fireball attack on Dark Charizard. As for the Eevee Chain Reaction questions, R & D is still researching into the “Counts as Evolving” rule. So hopefully we will have answers on it in the future. We have heard all about the concern for some upcoming Pokémon cards such as Team Rocket Trap, Chaos Gym, and Team Rocket Zapdos.
