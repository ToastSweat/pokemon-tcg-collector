Featuring Master_Trainers from Customer Service Thurs. June 1, 2000 Hey Trainers, it’s been a slow week as far as Poke-news is concerned. I just have some rulings from R&D for you from issues you asked about last week, so I’ll post those now and on the hour. Once again, MT Galen will be helping me out. OK, here are all the rulings I double-checked on for you from last week. Q: Can energy that has been changed to Fire energy with Charizard’s Energy Burn be moved by TR Charmander’s Gather Firepower? A: Yes, energy changed in this way will be Fire energy till the end of the turn, you can use TR Charmander’s power to move this energy. Q: If you attach a Plus Power to your active Pokémon and then retreat and then advance that Pokémon, will the Plus Power still be in effect? A: Yes, it will. Q: If you use TR Porygon’s Conversion 1 to change a Ditto’s(copying TR Porygon) weakness to Grass and then you retreat TR Porygon and advance a Scyther, will the converted weakness still be in effect on Ditto? A: Yes, any effects of attacks on Ditto will remain. Q: Can Ditto(copying a Gengar) repeatedly retreat and advance so that he can do Gengar’s Curse over and over? A: Yes, every time Ditto advances and copies Gengar he can activate the Curse power. On to the chat!
