 FAQs Continued: Click on each spoiler to view entire log: Pokémon Tournament and League FAQ - Circa 2000 Pokémon Tournament and League FAQ ANNOUNCEMENTS Here is lots of info on the upcoming Super Trainer Showdown. When: July 22,2000 10 am- 7 PM Where: The Queen Mary Long Beach, CA. What is it? A Swiss Style Invitation-only tournament for 3 age division: age 10 and under, 11-14 years old, and 15+ and up. How do I earn an invitation? There will be qualifying tournaments at each stop of the Pokémon Mall Tour, which is listed on our web site. The top 10 ranked DCI Pokémon players will get an automatic invite. There will be International Invitations in each country to be earned/given out. And lastly, ANYONE who has earned 3 or more badges in the Pokémon league can take part! Other stuff: There is no admission to take part and there are lots of cool prizes to be given, including the winner of each age group will be given the “Master Trainer Champion” title! We are working on East Coast stops for the Pokémon Mall Tour, which should include Qualifying events for the Super Trainer Showdown in July in Long Beach, CA. Remember though, if you earn 3 badges through the Pokémon League, you can also be in it. We are aware of several misprints in the Team Rocket set. Any misprints that DON’T effect game play (such as Arbok’s 23000 copyright, Rapidash missing the word “length”, and the non-foil Dark FDragonite incorrectly numbered as 5out of 82, and Dark Raichu’s incorrect “Put Raichu on Pikachu” instead of Dark Raichu) will all be corrected in future print runs. The misprints that DO affect game play (The non-foil Dark Vileplume having Fighting as its Weakness and Grimer having a Poison Gas attack that puts the other Pokémon Asleep) will NOT be corrected. The game play effect misprints will be played as is, on the cards. When playing in an English League or Tournament, you will play these cards as is. If playing in an International event, then the cards will be played, as they originally should have been. This will be announced at those events beforehand. Pat and I (Mike) and our boss (Mike) will be at the Mall Tour Stop at the Sea-Tac Mall this weekend as well. Feel free to stop on by and show us your deck and try and stop Boss Mike’s “fearsome” Mr. Yuk deck! The STS Invitational Tournament (main event) will include three age categories: 10 & under, 11-14, and 15 and up. You must bring your badge book with three stamps, or invitation from a tournament win to participate in the main event. The event is Swiss format. Every player in this tournament will receive a minimum gift of a tee shirt and Tournament certificate. Three first place winners (one from each age division) will receive a Gold Medal, 32 boosters, and a “Master Trainer Champion” Event Jacket and Cap. Three-second place winners (one from each age division) will receive a Silver Medal, 28 boosters, and an Event Jacket and Cap. Six third place winners (one from each age division) will receive a Bronze Medal, 20 boosters and an Event Jacket and Cap. Twelve fourth place finishers (four from each division will receive a Tournament Plaque, 16 boosters, and an Event Cap. Twenty-four fifth place winners (eight from each division) will receive a Tournament Certificate and 12 boosters. Forty-eight sixth place winners (16 from each division) will receive a Tournament Certificate and 8 boosters. Four hundred Sixteen runners up will each receive a Tournament Certificate. The 10 and under and 11-14 categories will be required to play with Base set and Jungle cards only. The 15 and up category can use cards up to and including the Team Rocket set. There will also be King of the Hill Tournaments and various other side events. There will be NO admission fees charged for this event. Mall Tour changes: The June 24-25th Mall Tour stop in Oklahoma City has been changed to Houston, TX. The correct location for this event will be: The Woodlands Mall, 1201 Lake Woodlands Dr., and Houston Most of you have probably already heard that the STS format changed for the 2 youngest age groups.
