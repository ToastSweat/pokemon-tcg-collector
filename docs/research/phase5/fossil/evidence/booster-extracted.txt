Booster packs have contained varying amounts of cards over the years. From the first Base Set through to Neo Destiny expansion, internationally released boosters contained 11 cards. Beginning with the e-Card Series and continuing through the entirety of the EX Series, boosters had only 9 cards. Since the release of the first Diamond & Pearl expansion, these consistently had 10 cards.
