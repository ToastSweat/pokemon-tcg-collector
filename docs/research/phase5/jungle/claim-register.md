# English Jungle Unlimited — research and claim register

Status: **data-only candidate; model and completion target NOT APPROVED**. Researched 2026-10-31. Scope proposal: English, standard set-symbol Jungle Unlimited with the 1999 reference identity `jungle-en-unlimited1999`; not every product ever labeled Jungle. The date in that proposed internal ID is not a verified manufacturing date for every copy. No generator, runtime, approved Base Set model, save, or original media has been changed.

## Evidence standard

- **Documented** means explicitly stated in retrieved text/metadata, not an assertion of factory authentication. Contemporary manufacturer statements reproduced by a later community member remain a transcription with an unverified chain to the original archive.
- **Corroborated metadata** means independently retrieved, separately maintained public datasets agree; common upstream borrowing has not been ruled out. These are not two independent physical samples.
- **Reported observation** is a named/threaded collector report, not an observation made by this researcher and not a measured population frequency.
- **Estimate/proposal** is a modeling choice awaiting approval; **unknown** means this retrieval did not establish it. No pack-opening video was watched, decoded, or counted.

Full retrieval headers, requested/final URLs, actual titles and UTC times are in `*.retrieval.json`. Raw HTML/JSON and text derivatives are retained. `evidence-quotes.json` binds verified verbatim quotes to their snapshot paths and hashes. `membership-comparison.json` contains every card's two-source normalized metadata and third-source name comparison.

The complete 64-entry metadata reconciliation agrees between the JSON dataset and the separate encyclopedia table; the third number/name list also matches after the declared spelling aliases.[1][2][3]

The retrieved English wrapper captions identify Wigglytuff, Scyther and Flareon.[3]

Pack guidance reports seven commons, one rare and three uncommons, while physical wrapper-relative orientation remains unestablished here.[16]

## Concrete provisional model proposal — NOT APPROVED, NOT IMPLEMENTED

This is a reviewable model alternative, not a `RuleData` definition or an instruction to change the accepted game.

1. Select only the 64 proposed standard-symbol English Unlimited identities in the candidate. Keep completion status `proposed-not-approved` and exclude the hazards in J06–J11.
2. Eleven cards: seven commons (including common Trainer Poké Ball as an ordinary common-pool member), one rare position that is either nonholo rare or holo rare, then three uncommons in a **nominal reveal convention**. Zero Energy. Evidence and orientation limits: J12–J16.
3. Tentative holo branch probability **1/3**; complementary nonholo branch **2/3**. These are approximation parameters, not exact selected-run factory odds. Under this proposal the two branches are mutually exclusive and the card total remains eleven. Evidence limits: J17.
4. Fallback **equal weights within each nominal rarity pool**, with no replacement within the seven commons and within the three uncommons. This is an intentionally simplified option for review, demonstrably not established historical sheet weighting. Consequence: it suppresses actual unequal marginal frequencies and sheet-neighbor correlations. A sheet-weighted alternative is blocked on numeric evidence and batch selection; do not fabricate those numbers. Evidence limits: J18–J20.
5. Treat repeated loose-pack draws as independent only for a provisional loose-pack simulation; allow repeats across packs. Do not call a group of generated packs a historically collated sealed box. No pity, guarantees of new collection entries, or rerolls.
6. Do not commit a physical stack orientation until direct orientation evidence exists. Accepting a nominal reveal convention would be accepting an approximation, not resolving J15. Wrapper A/B/C selection is presentation-only and must remain outside generation RNG.

**Consequential decisions still needed:** whether the ordinary 64 standard printings are the physical completion target; whether the explicit approximate 1/3 / uniform-pool / within-pool uniqueness / independent-loose-pack model is acceptable; and whether a nominal order is acceptable pending physical orientation proof. Temporary art itself already has approval and is not re-requested. If any model assumptions are rejected, retain this candidate as research data rather than silently choosing a different rule.

- `catalog-validation.json`: real retained-source reconciliation and candidate reread; 64 canonical numbers/unique IDs, 16 of each rarity, 16 holo plus 48 nonholo, 63 Pokémon plus one Trainer, no Energy. Candidate eligibility flags are policy, not source fields.
- `authoring-final-tests.log`: five Python tests pass; includes twelve mutated candidate subcases and a mutated real source rejection. `tdd-01` through `tdd-05` preserve actual RED exits and subsequent GREEN exits (the fifth cycle catches Python boolean/integer equality masking JSON type drift).
- `domain-validation.log` / `.json`: actual Unity-provided Mono compiler and production `Data.cs` / `Catalog.cs`, warnings as errors; both processes exited 0 and **five checks passed**, including real catalog loading and duplicate-ID rejection. Source pins unchanged during this run.
- No Unity scene/build/playthrough, new-set generator, RNG distribution, save migration, mixed-set persistence or final-art correctness is claimed. Those are later implementation acceptance criteria, not validations made true by a data-only candidate.
