Jungle






Original Release Date:
 June 16th 1999

Amount of Cards
: 64

Theme Decks
:
   
Power Reserve 



   
Water Blast 




The Jungle set was the first expansion to the Pokémon Trading Card game. It introduced various aspects including cards that have both Holographic and Non-Holographic versions. It also only contained one Trainer card and so was very dependant on Pokémon for the expansion. It included many remaining Kanto Pokémon and the Pokémon within it were mostly Pokémon those would expect to see in a Jungle.
