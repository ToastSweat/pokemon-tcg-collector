## Scope and evidence policy

Target: English, shadowed, ©1999 Wizards Base Set Unlimited; exclude Shadowless, ordinary First Edition, ©1999–2000 and other releases. Machamp's stamped starter issue is a separate completion-scope decision, never a booster draw. Identification must respect card kind; a Pokémon illustration-shadow test does not apply universally to Trainers/Energy.[8][9]

All sources below were accessed 2026-10-30. Full titles, exact URLs, short evidence quotations and limitations are in the linked findings documents; project-local `citation-ledger.json` owns numeric source identities. “Documented” describes the source/artifact, not automatic certification of factory history. Opening observations and retrospective collector models are labelled accordingly.
