## Decisions / scope boundary

- Owner defaults: opening balance **$100.00**, contribution suggestion **$10.00**, both configurable. Suggestions do not automatically increase confirmed funds.
- Money remains a manual record. No banking, PayPal, automatic transfers, account login or purchases. Spending does not mark physical cards owned.
- First-time initialization marks existing opened packs seen without inventing past contributions. Existing ledgers reconcile any opened-pack gap after restart using recorded transaction identities.
- Existing accepted visuals, models, music, card-back/wrapper behavior and search providers are not reopened for redesign.
- The final editable source should point to the final startup scene rather than the historical Phase 2 bootstrap. Apply that only after genuine scene generation/validation and before freezing the final build.
- Financial integration feasibility, extra sets, automatic market prices, a new main menu and other expansions are **future-only / deferred**. They are not blockers for this private release.
- Do not delete historical players, saves, supplied originals or retained failed evidence as cleanup. Keep development artifacts out of the handoff package instead.
